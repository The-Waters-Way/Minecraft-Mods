# Minecraft Mod / Shader / Resource Pack Dump:

# By Rowan

# About:

This is a collection of mods, shaders, and resource packs that I’ve gathered and used over time. Instead of letting them sit unused, I decided to share them so others can enjoy them too.

Everything in this pack is **free to use**.
You’re welcome to:

* Use it in your own worlds
* Modify it to fit your style
* Share it with friends
* Include it in personal modpacks

# Notes:

* Some content may belong to their original creators — full credit goes to them.
* If you recognize your work here and want it removed or credited differently, feel free to reach out.
* Performance may vary depending on your system, especially with shaders enabled.

# Installation:

1. Download the files
2. Place:

   * Mods → `.minecraft/mods`
   * Resource Packs → `.minecraft/resourcepacks`
   * Shader Packs → `.minecraft/shaderpacks`
3. (Optional) edit config via Mod Menu.

# Final Message

This pack is just something I put together for fun and convenience.
I hope you find something in here you like and that it improves your Minecraft experience.
